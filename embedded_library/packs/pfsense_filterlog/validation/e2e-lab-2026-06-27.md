## Action taken from this attempt

The pfsense pack-specific harness and the reusable remote-lab compose helper were hardened so SC4S health requires a real `syslog-ng` process and a stats response that is not the missing-control-socket error:

```text
pgrep -x syslog-ng >/dev/null 2>&1 && syslog-ng-ctl stats 2>&1 | grep -qv 'Error connecting control socket'
```

Regression tests were added to prove both the standard remote lab compose output and the pfSense pack harness carry this stricter healthcheck. A post-hardening lab retry timed out waiting for the SC4S service to become healthy; `docker compose ps` showed Splunk healthy and SC4S stuck in `health: starting`, which is the intended fail-closed behavior for this blocker rather than proceeding to fixture replay.

```text
sechub-pfsense-filterlog-e2e-sc4s-1     sc4s      Up 9 minutes (health: starting)   0.0.0.0:20030->20030/tcp
sechub-pfsense-filterlog-e2e-splunk-1   splunk    Up 9 minutes (healthy)            0.0.0.0:18000->8000/tcp, 0.0.0.0:18088->8088/tcp, 0.0.0.0:18089->8089/tcp
```

Future runs should fail closed at SC4S health if the daemon aborts, rather than proceeding to fixture replay and reporting only an OpenSSL connection reset.
